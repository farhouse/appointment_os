## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The working day is visible at once. The dashboard refuses the interchangeable metric-card grid: the schedule is the product's dominant operational surface.

**OWN-WORLD:** Bright cool canvas, white work surfaces, deep ink navigation, mineral-blue actions, eucalyptus success, amber attention and coral alerts. Fine rules and 4-8px corners replace decorative shadows and oversized cards.

**STORY:** An owner or manager selects a branch, understands today's load and revenue, sees every professional's schedule, catches exceptions and acts without navigating away.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Stable left navigation and compact top bar frame a date-led header. Four inline metrics sit above a 09:00-19:00 calendar with one column per professional and a current-time line. Alerts and next actions occupy a narrower right rail. Quick notes close the visible work surface.

**FORM:** Hybrid of the category-standard light operations shell and the assigned editorial calendar structure; user-approved after the direction round with seed key `89e7daaa`.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
